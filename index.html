How to Play Last Match

The Goal

Outsmart your opponent by striking matches from the board. You can play against Piro or challenge a friend on the same device.

Taking Your Turn

  • Strike off any number of matches from one row.
  • Swipe across matches with your finger, or click and drag with your mouse.
  • Drag backward to deselect matches. Drag all the way back to cancel your move.
  • Release to strike the selected matches.
  • Players alternate turns until no matches remain.

Last Match Wins or Loses?

Choose your rule in Game Setup:

  • Last Match Wins: Strike the final match to win.
  • Last Match Loses: Strike the final match and lose.

The final move may strike one or several matches.

Split On or Off?

Split Off: Remaining matches slide together after each move. You can strike matches anywhere within one row.

Split On: Removing matches can create separate groups within a row. Those groups stay apart, and you may strike matches from only one group per turn. Matches selected within a group must be consecutive.

Game Setup

  • 1 Player: Challenge Piro at Beginner or Expert difficulty.
  • 2 Players: Play against a friend on the same screen.
  • Board Config: Choose 3–5–7, 4–6–8, 5–7–9, or Random.
  • Split: Turn splitting On or Off.
  • Last Match: Choose whether taking the final match wins or loses.

Turns and Scores

The highlighted name on the scoreboard shows whose turn it is. Orange represents Player 1; blue represents Player 2 or Piro.

The numbers beside the names show games won. The starting player alternates each new game.

Pause or Cancel

Press Pause to temporarily stop the game.

  • Resume: Continue where you left off.
  • Cancel Game: End the current game without changing the scores.

A Little Strategy

There is a mathematical strategy behind Last Match. Watch how the board changes and look for patterns. Every move matters!
